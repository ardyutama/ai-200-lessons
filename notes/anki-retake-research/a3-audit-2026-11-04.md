## Learn-page quirks (the Notes are correct; worth knowing in the exam)

- The AKS network-services page describes ExternalName with LoadBalancer-style text; kubernetes.io is canonical (a CNAME alias, no proxying).
- The Container Apps ingress page spells the client-certificate value "Required" in one column and "Require" in prose; the ARM enum is `require`.
- A Functions training module has the blob-trigger poison-blob and receipt descriptions backwards; the reference article is right, so the Note stands.
- The Durable Functions overview was rewritten in 2026; its patterns moved to the Durable Task hub.
- The Service Bus trigger article still names the 4.x key `maxAutoRenewDuration`.
